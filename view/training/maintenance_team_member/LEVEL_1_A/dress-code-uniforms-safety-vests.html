Dress Code/Uniforms/Safety Vests

Purpose: PSA is committed to maintaining a professional and safe work environment. A consistent dress code and uniform policy help ensure employee safety, promote team unity, and uphold our company’s professional image. This policy outlines the expectations for appropriate work attire.

Dress Code Guidelines:
1. Uniform Requirement: Employees are required to wear the designated company safety uniform during work hours. This includes:
- Company-branded safety vests (provided by the company)
- Durable work pants or shorts (as approved by management)
- Hiking/work boots preferred with slip-resistant for safety

2. Personal Grooming: Employees should maintain a neat and clean appearance while on duty. Clothing must be clean, in good condition, and free from excessive wear and tear.

3. Protective Gear: Employees working in hazardous areas must wear the appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), including gloves, safety glasses, and high-visibility vests as required.

4. Considerations: Employees are encouraged to dress appropriately for the weather while adhering to safety and uniform requirements. Outerwear (coats) worn during colder month covered with vests.

5. Prohibited Attire: The following clothing items are not permitted:
- Open-toed shoes or sandals
- Clothing with offensive or inappropriate graphics or language
- Excessively baggy or tight-fitting clothing that poses a safety hazard
- Jewelry that may interfere with work or pose a safety risk

Exceptions & Special Considerations:

-Employees with religious or medical requirements for attire should discuss accommodations with management.,br> -Office staff may follow a business casual dress code unless otherwise required to wear company uniforms.

Policy Enforcement: Failure to comply with the dress code policy may result in corrective action, including verbal or written warnings. Repeated violations may lead to further disciplinary action. By adhering to this dress code policy, employees contribute to a professional, safe, and cohesive work environment.
