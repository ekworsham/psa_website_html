Job Description

Job Title: ProScapes of Atlanta Team Member

Purpose: Safely install and maintain commercial and residential grounds while meeting company quality and safety standards.

Essential Functions:
- Work outdoors in varying weather conditions, including heat, cold, dry, and rainy environments.
- Participate in required training and safety programs.
- Inspect plants, operate power equipment, and remain alert to changing site conditions.
- Lift, carry, and transport materials and equipment over variable terrain throughout the workday.
- Use hand tools and mechanized equipment such as mowers, trimmers, blowers, chainsaws, tillers, and water tanks.
- Read and follow pesticide labels, mixing instructions, and application procedures.
- Perform pruning, cleanup, loading, unloading, and daily equipment checks.
- Maintain a clean work area, follow company procedures, and present a professional appearance at all times.

Qualifications:
- High school diploma or equivalent preferred.
- Ability to lift up to 50 lbs. and work in outdoor conditions.

Reports To: ProScapes of Atlanta Supervisor

Work Schedule: Approximately 40 hours per week, generally from 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Occasional Saturday work and overtime may be required during peak seasons, inclement weather, or special projects.

Acknowledgment: I have read and understand the job description and confirm that I am able to perform these duties.
