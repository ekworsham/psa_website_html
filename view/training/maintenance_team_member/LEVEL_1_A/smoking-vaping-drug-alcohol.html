Smoking, Vaping, Drugs and Alcohol

Purpose: To ensure a safe, professional, and respectful work environment for all employees, clients, and visitors. ProScapes of Atlanta maintains a strict no smoking, no vaping, no drug, and no alcohol policy on all company vehicles and client properties.

Policy Overview:
  • To Smoking, vaping, or the use of any tobacco, nicotine, or similar products is strictly prohibited:
    • Inside or around company vehicles and equipment
    • On any client property or job site
    • During work hours while representing ProScapes of Atlanta
  • The use, possession, or influence of illegal drugs, controlled substances, or alcohol is strictly prohibited during working hours or while operating company vehicles and equipment.

Client Contract Compliance:
  • ProScapes of Atlanta has signed agreements with clients that require compliance with no smoking, no vaping, and no substance use policies on their properties.
  • Violating this policy not only breaches company policy but also violates contractual agreements with our clients and can jeopardize ongoing and future business relationships.

Employee Responsibility:
  • All employees must adhere to this policy without exception.
  • It is each employee’s responsibility to ensure:
    • No smoking or vaping occurs in or near company trucks, equipment, or client job sites.
    • No possession or use of drugs or alcohol during work hours.
    • o Professional representation of ProScapes of Atlanta at all times.

Disciplinary Action:
  • Any employee found violating this policy will be subject to immediate disciplinary action, up to and including:
    • Written warning or suspension
    • Termination of employment
    • Possible reporting to local authorities if illegal substances are involved

Company Support:
  • ProScapes of Atlanta encourages a healthy, safe, and productive work environment.
  • Employees seeking assistance with smoking cessation or substance-related issues may confidentially request information about available support programs.
