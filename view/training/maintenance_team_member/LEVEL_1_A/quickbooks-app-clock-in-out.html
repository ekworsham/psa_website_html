LEVEL 1 - A | QuickBooks Workforce app

QuickBooks Workforce app

Purpose: To train employees to effectively use the QuickBooks Workforce app to clock in and out, track breaks, and manage their timesheets to include hands-on training.

Essential Functions:

Basics:

  • MANDITORY for submitting time for payroll processing
  • Receive an invitation from ProScapes for QuickBooks Workforce App
  • Overview of QuickBooks Workforce
  • Benefits: payroll accuracy, compliance, ease of use

Requirements:

  • Smartphone (iOS or Android)
  • Stable internet connection or use of office Wi-Fi.
  • Company login credentials

Set Up App:

  • Step 1: Download and install the QuickBooks Workforce app.
  • Step 2: Log in using the provided credentials.
  • Step 3: Set up notifications and location permissions. Enable GPS tracking for location-based clock-ins and outs.

Clocking In and Out

Clocking In:

  • AT THE OFFICE, Open the app.
  • Bottom of screen, choose the “Track Time” icon.
  • Select the correct job (first job for the day):
    • DO NOT SELECT “BILLABLE”.
  • Select “Service Item”:
    • Select “Maintenance”.
  • Tap Clock In.
  • Verify that the app confirms the clock-in time.

    • Clocking Out:

  • AT THE OFFICE, Tap “Clock Out”.
  • Review your work hours for accuracy.
  • Always check for confirmation to ensure time is logged correctly.
  • In the event that you forget to clock in/out, get with your supervisor.
